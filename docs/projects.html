Projects & Research Initiatives

An overview of my active roles, responsibilities, and technical contributions across various grant-funded studies, applied initiatives, and projects at TCU and the Institute of Behavioral Research (IBR).

Grant-Funded Studies & Lab Initiatives (IBR)

ACTION Study

Justice & Public Health

Reentry is a turning point—and the ACTION study tests new ways to connect people leaving prison to HIV and opioid treatment. The project seeks to close care gaps and support recovery.

Contributions & Focus: Actively involved in compiling and drafting various manuscripts. This includes leading an in-progress paper comparing machine learning algorithms to optimize substance use prediction and model performance variables within justice-involved populations.

BRIDGE Study

Community Deflection

The BRIDGE study tests how deep community engagement can make deflection programs stronger, more sustainable, and lifesaving.

Contributions & Focus: Contributing to the data architecture and mapping systems of the study. Responsibilities include the development of an integrated community metric dashboard alongside building a structured mapping protocol to standardize deflection program processes across multiple active research sites.

VALUES Study

AI & Resilience

The VALUES study is creating an AI-powered tool to boost resilience and well-being, offering personalized support for positive change.

Contributions & Focus: Aided technical workflows by evaluating and testing qualitative AI coding techniques. Processed text data extracted from post-incarceration interviews to isolate and map core community transition values.

Federal Bureau of Prisons (BOP) Partnership

Program Evaluation

Inside federal prisons, IBR is evaluating substance use and anger management programs that shape how people prepare for life after release.

Contributions & Focus: Collaborating on multiple papers focusing on large-scale institutional program evaluations. Leveraging core data science experience to clean complex data arrays, run comparative analyses, and implement predictive modeling workflows to evaluate long-term psychological and behavioral outcomes.

SOAR Lab Mentorship

Leadership & Mentorship

Contributions & Focus: Serve as a graduate mentor to undergraduate research assistants navigating community-based studies at the intersection of public health and criminal justice. Supported student-led pipelines on projects evaluating community Narcan attitudes and the Amen & Awareness faith-based health referral initiatives.

Translational Platforms & Applied Systems

Center for Translation to Practice (TTP)

EdTech & Implementation

The Center for Translation to Practice takes IBR’s mission a step further—helping agencies and practitioners apply research-based strategies directly in their programs.

Contributions & Focus: Design and manage professional training frameworks for standardized IBR evaluation assessments. Formulate interactive digital curricula and consult on translation pipelines to smoothly port evidence-based interventions into field practice via software platforms including LearnWorlds and Articulate.

TCU Neeley AI Forward Initiative

Psychometrics & Grant Writing

The Neeley AI Forward Initiative at TCU is a strategic program designed to integrate applied artificial intelligence into undergraduate and graduate business education.

Contributions & Focus: Conceptualized and led the development of an original psychometric research project in collaboration with faculty and an industry partner (Career-Ready AI / Sunbonn). Authored a full SERC grant proposal — including project description, methodology, detailed budget allocations, budget justification text, and broader impact statements — submitted for funding through TCU's College of Science and Engineering. Collaborated directly with a faculty advisor and industry contact to structurally align scale metrics with the Career-Ready AI platform's existing infrastructure and the broader NEELI AI Forward initiative at TCU's Neeley School of Business.
