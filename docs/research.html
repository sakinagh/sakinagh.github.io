Research & Publications

As a Graduate Research Assistant at the Institute of Behavioral Research (IBR) and a long-standing researcher within the SOAR Lab, my work spans health psychology, justice-involved populations, and technology implementation. My methodology leverages data management, predictive statistical workflows, and machine learning techniques to evaluate behavioral trends and health service paradigms.
